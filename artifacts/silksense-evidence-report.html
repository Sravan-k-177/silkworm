1. Product and reused foundation

React, TypeScript and Vite provide the UI; i18next provides bundled English and Telugu copy. Dexie/IndexedDB stores device records and media. Express, SQLite and Express sessions provide the account API. Torchvision MobileNetV3-Small supplies ImageNet-pretrained convolutional features; a supervised classification head is trained on the audited larval image source. ONNX Runtime executes the same frozen graph on the server and in browser WebAssembly.

The product contribution is the connection between uncertain observations, image evidence, preventive care, follow-up and recorded outcomes. No new convolutional architecture is claimed. A low image score does not erase reported deaths or environmental concerns. Self-reported fresh feed is accompanied by a suspect-feed sensitivity scenario.

2. Internal model evidence

The larval source is Mungase and Chiwhane, Silkworm_Diseases_Dataset v1 (CC BY 4.0). The acquisition contains 5,529 images. The audit retained 3,843 source-family representatives and quarantined conflicting groups. Training, selection, calibration and test partitions are separate.

The backbone remains frozen; this is transfer learning of a classification head. Overlap is a scene condition, not a disease. The separate mulberry leaf classifier provides experimental feed-image review and cannot establish larval health. The overall low/medium/high priority score uses transparent engineering rules, not a trained predictor of future mortality.

  • Dataset folder labels are not independently confirmed diagnoses.
  • No linked onset, environmental or mortality data: no early-warning lead time or yield benefit established.
  • One source, heuristic duplicate groups; hidden shared specimens/capture sessions and background confounding remain possible.
  • Overlap is not a disease; its output is withheld from health interpretation.
  • Temperature calibration is internal to this source; not validated field confidence.
  • Unsupported objects may still receive high scores. No universal out-of-distribution detector.
  • Random image-group holdout is not an independent farm or regional validation.

3. Verification and evaluation pathway

See the repository testing record for exact suite counts and commands. Browser tests exercise backend/device probability parity, camera permission handling, offline inference, capture/reload/sync, accessible screens, Telugu workflows and mobile widths. Account tests exercise anonymous rejection, cross-farm isolation, review permissions, logout, offline expiry and user workspace separation. Python evaluator tests check leakage rejection and parity with the frozen graph. These are software checks, not field-effectiveness studies.

The external evaluator consumes an independently supplied manifest with farm, batch, capture group, source, licence and label method. It rejects empty data and exact overlap with the acquisition corpus and reports confusion matrices, per-farm results, calibration error, score abstention and farm-cluster uncertainty. It does not tune the network or thresholds. Exact hashes cannot establish full acquisition independence; near-duplicate and annotation review remain necessary.

Prospective warning validation needs independently timed onset, a predeclared prediction horizon, negative follow-up cohorts and a frozen model. Yield-benefit claims need a comparison design that controls baseline differences and complete outcome denominators. Logging an intervention and a later harvest is insufficient to infer benefit.
